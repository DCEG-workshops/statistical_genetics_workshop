Data Used:

  • Summary statistics from Wang et. al. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37945903/
  • PRS evaluation: subset of data from https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/projects/gap/cgi-bin/study.cgi?study_id=phs001081.v2.p2 and will be updated and available for the Multiethnic Cohort on dbGaP.
