Dr. Kai Yu is a senior investigator in the Biostatistics Branch of DCEG. He received his Ph.D. in biostatistics from the University of Pittsburgh in 2000 and completed postdoctoral training in statistical genetics at Stanford University. He joined NCI in 2005 as a tenure-track investigator and was awarded scientific tenure by the NIH and appointed senior investigator in 2012. In 2009, he received an NIH Merit Award for developing creative statistical methods for genetic epidemiologic studies, and he is an elected Fellow of the American Statistical Association. Dr. Yu's research addresses a wide variety of statistically and computationally challenging problems that arise in the design and analysis of modern high-dimensional genetic and molecular epidemiologic studies.
