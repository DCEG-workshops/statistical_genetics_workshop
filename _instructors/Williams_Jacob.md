Jacob Williams, Ph.D., has been a postdoctoral fellow in DCEG's Biostatistics Branch since July 2023. His training includes a doctorate in statistics from Virginia Tech and a bachelor's degree in statistics from Montana State University. His doctoral research developed Bayesian approaches to variable selection in ultra-high-dimensional linear mixed models for genome-wide association studies. At DCEG, he develops methods that combine information from rare and common genomic variants to improve polygenic risk prediction, with mentorship from Haoyu Zhang, Ph.D., and Xihao Li, Ph.D.
