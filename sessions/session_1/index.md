**Date:** September 9, 2026

**Instructors:** Peter Kraft, Xueyao Wu

### Topics Covered

The first session in the Workshop will include an introductory overview of the course and genetic epidemiology, a review of very basic molecular and population genetics, and a hands-on tutorial on managing genome-wide association study (GWAS) data and performing simple quality control and assessment analyses.

### Recommended Readings/Videos

- Review of human molecular genetics
    - Sue Ingles, [Basic Concepts of Molecular Genetics](https://doi.org/10.1093/oso/9780195159394.003.0002), from Duncan Thomas' *Statistical Methods in Genetic Epidemiology*
- Overview of GWAS and post-GWAS analyses
    - Uffelmann et al. (2021), [Genome-wide association studies](https://doi.org/10.1038/s43586-021-00056-9)
