**Date:** October 14, 2026

**Instructors:** Jianxin Shi, Kai Yu

## Topics Covered

This session introduces post-GWAS analyses that connect genetic associations to gene expression and estimate the contribution of common genetic variants to trait variation. We will discuss how transcriptome-wide association studies (TWAS) integrate GWAS and gene expression data to identify associated genes, along with the challenges of interpreting these associations as evidence of causality. We will also cover SNP-based heritability estimation using GCTA and LD Score regression, including their data requirements, assumptions, and interpretation.
