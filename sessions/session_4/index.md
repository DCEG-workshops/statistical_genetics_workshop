**Instructor:** Diptavo Dutta

## Topics Covered

This session introduces statistical fine-mapping to prioritize candidate causal variants at GWAS loci and colocalization to assess whether two association signals are consistent with a shared causal variant. We will discuss linkage disequilibrium, posterior inclusion probabilities, credible sets, and the interpretation of colocalization evidence.

## Practical Tutorial

The [Fine Mapping and Colocalization Lab (`04_finemapping_colocalization.Rmd`)](https://github.com/DCEG-workshops/statgen_workshop_tutorial/blob/main/src/04_finemapping_colocalization.Rmd) uses SuSiE to fine-map the FGFR2 region and `coloc` to compare GWAS and gene-expression association signals.
