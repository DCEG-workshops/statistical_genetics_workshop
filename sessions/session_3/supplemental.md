## Optional Reading After the Session

The following readings are optional resources for further study after the session:

1. **Gene–environment interactions in human health.** Herrera-Luis et al. (2024). *Nature Reviews Genetics*, 25:768–784. A review of gene–environment interactions in human health. [Publisher page](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41576-024-00731-z).
2. **A data model for population descriptors in genomic research.** Khan et al. (2025). *American Journal of Human Genetics*, 112:1504–1514. A data model to operationalize population descriptors in genomics research. [Publisher page](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ajhg.2025.05.011) · [Free full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12256887/).
3. **Getting genetic ancestry right for science and society.** Lewis et al. (2022). *Science*, 376(6590):250–252. Additional information on genetic ancestry and ethical considerations in genomics research. [Publisher page](https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.abm7530) · [Free full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10135340/).
