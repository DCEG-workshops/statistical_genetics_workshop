The linked highlights PDF contains four pages. [Full report](https://doi.org/10.17226/26902).
2. **Kraft & Hunter: read pages 1–7 of the chapter.** [*The challenge of assessing complex gene–environment and gene–gene interactions*](https://academic.oup.com/book/34832/chapter-abstract/297763024).
